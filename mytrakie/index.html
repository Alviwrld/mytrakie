    How it works

    1. Scan 01

      You paste a number

      Trakie recognises UPS, FedEx, USPS, DHL, Amazon and about forty others from the number alone, then asks the carrier what it knows.

    2. Scan 02

      Your courier loads up

      Every time a new scan appears, he walks over, picks the box up and climbs into whatever's going your way — painted in the carrier's colours.

    3. Scan 03

      You watch it move

      Ground legs go by truck, long hops go by plane. The map keeps refreshing on its own, so leave the tab open.
